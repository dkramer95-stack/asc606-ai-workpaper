## 1. PARTIES AND SCOPE

1.1 This Statement of Work No. 1 (this **SOW**) is entered into under the Master Subscription Agreement dated July 1, 2026 (the **Agreement**) and is governed by it.
1.2 Provider will perform the Implementation Services described in Section 4 and will provide the Hosted Service described in Section 5 as a single integrated deployment for Customer's clinical operations.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

2.1 **Implementation Services** means the configuration, migration, integration, testing, and cutover services described in Section 4.
2.2 **Hosted Service** means Provider's hosted care-coordination platform, as configured for Customer by the Implementation Services.
2.3 **Custom Configuration** means the workflow configuration, forms, roles, and rules built for Customer during the Implementation Services.
2.4 **Go-Live Date** means the date on which the configured Hosted Service is first made available for Customer's productive use.
2.5 **Customer Data** means the data migrated from Customer's legacy systems.

## 3. TERM AND GO-LIVE

3.1 This SOW begins on July 1, 2026 and ends on June 30, 2029, co-terminous with the Initial Term of the Agreement.
3.2 Target Go-Live Date: October 1, 2026.
3.3 Hosted Service fees accrue from the Effective Date and are invoiced monthly in advance under Section 6.2.

## 4. IMPLEMENTATION SERVICES

4.1 **Scope.** Provider will design, configure, test, and deploy the Hosted Service for Customer, including: (a) custom workflow configuration for utilization review, prior authorization, and care-plan management; (b) data migration of up to 1,800,000 member records from Customer's legacy platform; (c) role and permission design; (d) an interface build to Customer's claims clearinghouse; (e) end-to-end testing and user training; and (f) cutover and stabilization support.
4.2 **Custom Workflow Configuration.** The Implementation Services are not a standard installation. Provider will build Customer-specific workflow logic, adjudication rules, forms, and data mappings that do not exist in Provider's standard product and that are configured for Customer's clinical and compliance requirements.
4.3 **Data Migration.** Provider will map, extract, transform, validate, and load Customer Data, including historical authorizations, clinical notes, and member eligibility history.

<!-- page 2 -->

4.4 **Significant customization.** The Implementation Services significantly customize the Hosted Service. The resulting Custom Configuration is integral to the Hosted Service and has no utility to Customer separately from the Hosted Service.
4.5 **Proprietary dependency.** The Implementation Services cannot be performed by another provider without access to Provider's proprietary environment, including its configuration toolset, source schemas, and deployment pipeline. No third party, including any systems integrator engaged by Customer, has access to that environment.
4.6 **Milestones.** Implementation is delivered in three phases: (a) design authorization, due August 14, 2026; (b) configured build and data migration, due September 18, 2026; and (c) cutover and stabilization, due October 30, 2026.
4.7 **No separate acceptance.** Customer does not accept the Implementation Services as a deliverable separate from the Hosted Service. Acceptance of the configured Hosted Service occurs only through the go-live readiness review in Section 5.4.

## 5. HOSTED SERVICE

5.1 Provider will host, operate, maintain, and support the configured Hosted Service for the remainder of the Term.
5.2 Provider will apply updates and patches to the Hosted Service and will maintain the Custom Configuration as part of the Hosted Service.
5.3 Provider will make the Hosted Service available at least 99.9% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
5.4 **Go-live readiness review.** Customer and Provider will jointly review the configured Hosted Service against the acceptance criteria in Exhibit B before the Go-Live Date.

## 6. FEES AND INVOICING

6.1 The fees payable under this SOW are as follows.

| Component | Amount | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Implementation Services | $600,000 | One-time fixed fee for the scope in Section 4 |
| Hosted Service | $1,200,000 | $33,333.33 per month for 36 months from July 1, 2026 |
| **Total SOW value** | **$1,800,000** | |

6.2 **Hosted Service billing.** The Hosted Service fee of $33,333.33 per month is invoiced monthly in advance commencing July 1, 2026 and continuing through June 2029.
6.3 **Implementation billing.** The $600,000 Implementation Fee is invoiced 50% on the Effective Date and 50% on the Go-Live Date. The billing milestones in this Section 6.3 are a payment schedule only and are not a measure of performance or of the transfer of control of any deliverable.
6.4 **No separate fees.** There are no additional fees for updates, standard support, or maintenance of the Custom Configuration during the Term.
6.5 **Taxes.** Fees exclude applicable taxes, which are Customer's responsibility.
6.6 **Refunds.** Fees are non-refundable except on termination for Provider's uncured material breach, in which case prepaid fees for periods after termination are refunded pro rata.

## 7. CHANGE ORDERS

7.1 Additional scope requires a written change order signed by both parties.
7.2 A change order may adjust the Implementation Fee and the delivery schedule, but does not change the Hosted Service fee unless it expressly states that it does.
