## EXHIBIT B — VOLUME REBATE SCHEDULE

### B-1. Purpose

This Exhibit B sets out the volume rebate available to Customer based on the annualized platform fees paid by Customer in each contract year. The rebate is consideration payable to Customer that varies based on Customer's level of fees and is an obligation of Provider, not a discretionary discount.

### B-2. Rebate tiers

| Annualized platform fees in the contract year | Rebate rate applied to annualized platform fees |
|---|---|
| Less than $300,000 | 0% (no rebate) |
| At least $300,000 but less than $350,000 | 2.0% |
| At least $350,000 | 3.0% |

### B-3. Rebate calculation

B-3.1 The rebate for a contract year equals the rebate rate for the applicable row of the table in Section B-2, multiplied by the annualized platform fees for that contract year.

B-3.2 **Annualized platform fees** means platform fees invoiced under this Order Form in the contract year, excluding taxes, one-time fees, and pass-through third-party charges, multiplied by a fraction the numerator of which is twelve (12) and the denominator of which is the number of months in the contract year.

B-3.3 Tiers are determined separately for each contract year and are not cumulative across contract years.

B-3.4 There is no cap on the aggregate rebate payable to Customer under this Exhibit B.

### B-4. Rebate payment

B-4.1 Customer's rebate accrues on the last day of each contract year and is paid in full within sixty (60) days after each contract anniversary.

B-4.2 The rebate is paid as a credit against the next invoice issued to Customer. If no invoice follows within the sixty-day period, Provider will pay the rebate by bank transfer.

B-4.3 The rebate is not a price adjustment, a prepayment, or a discount to the base platform fee. It is a separate amount payable to Customer each contract year.

### B-5. Customer estimate (informational)

B-5.1 The parties acknowledge that Customer's expected annualized platform fees for each contract year are $360,000.

B-5.2 On that stated expectation, the applicable rebate rate is 3.0% and the expected rebate is $10,800 per contract year, or $32,400 over the Term.

B-5.3 This Section B-5 records the parties' expectation only. The calculation in Section B-3 governs if actual fees differ.

### B-6. No set-off before accrual

B-6.1 Customer may not set off fees owed against a rebate until that rebate has accrued and become payable under Section B-4.
