## 2. PRODUCT LINES ORDERED

2.1 Customer orders the following three product lines (each, a **Product Line**) for the Term:

(a) **Platform** — Meridian Harbor Platform, Enterprise tier; 400 named users; unlimited workspaces; standard application programming interface access.

(b) **Premium Support** — 24x7x365 support coverage; Priority 1 response within one hour; named technical account manager; quarterly service reviews; annual disaster-recovery test.

(c) **Add-on Modules** — the Forecast Studio, Audit Trail Plus, and Multi-Currency Ledger modules; 400 named users per module.

2.2 Each Product Line is ordered for the full Term and may not be reduced or cancelled during the Term except as provided in Section 1.3.
