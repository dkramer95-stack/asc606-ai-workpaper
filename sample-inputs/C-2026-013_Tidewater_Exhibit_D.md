## EXHIBIT D — DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM

This Exhibit D forms part of the Master Subscription Agreement dated July 1, 2026 between Meridian Harbor Software, Inc. (the **Processor**) and Tidewater Freight Co. (the **Controller**) and applies to all Personal Data processed in connection with the Hosted Services.

### D-1. Definitions

D-1.1 **Applicable Data Protection Law** means the data protection and privacy laws applicable to the Personal Data processed under the Agreement.

D-1.2 **Personal Data** means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person that the Processor processes on behalf of the Controller in connection with the Hosted Services.

D-1.3 **Subprocessor** means any third party engaged by the Processor to process Personal Data.

D-1.4 **Breach** means a confirmed accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to Personal Data.

D-1.5 **Standard Contractual Clauses** means the model contractual clauses approved for the international transfer of Personal Data.

### D-2. Roles and scope of processing

D-2.1 The Controller determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data. The Processor processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Controller, including with regard to transfers of Personal Data to a third country, unless required to do otherwise by law.

D-2.2 The Processor will not sell Personal Data, use it for advertising, or combine it with personal data from other customers.

D-2.3 The subject matter, duration, nature, and purpose of the processing, and the categories of data subjects and Personal Data, are set out in Annex D-2.

<!-- page 2 -->

### D-3. Data location and international transfers

D-3.1 Personal Data is hosted in the Processor's production data centres located in the United States (Commonwealth of Virginia and State of Oregon).

D-3.2 The Processor will not transfer Personal Data outside the United States without the Controller's prior written consent, other than as required by law, and will implement the Standard Contractual Clauses for any permitted transfer.

D-3.3 Backups are stored in the same regions and under the same technical and organisational measures as production data.

D-3.4 The Processor will notify the Controller of any change to the primary hosting region at least thirty (30) days in advance.

### D-4. Subprocessors

D-4.1 The Controller grants general written authorisation for the Processor to engage the Subprocessors listed in Annex D-4.

D-4.2 The Processor will give the Controller at least thirty (30) days' prior notice of the addition or replacement of a Subprocessor. The Controller may object on reasonable data-protection grounds, and the parties will discuss the objection in good faith.

D-4.3 The Processor remains fully liable to the Controller for the acts and omissions of its Subprocessors.

D-4.4 The Processor will impose data-protection obligations on each Subprocessor that are no less protective than those in this Exhibit D.

<!-- page 3 -->

### D-5. Security measures

D-5.1 The Processor will implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures described in Annex D-5, including encryption of Personal Data at rest and in transit, role-based access control, and audit logging.

D-5.2 The Processor will restrict access to Personal Data to personnel who need the access to perform the Services and who are bound by confidentiality obligations.

D-5.3 The Processor will test and review the security of the Hosted Services at least annually and will make a summary of the results available to the Controller on request.

### D-6. Breach notification

D-6.1 The Processor will notify the Controller without undue delay and in any event within forty-eight (48) hours after becoming aware of a Breach.

D-6.2 The notification will describe the nature of the Breach, the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned, the likely consequences, and the measures taken or proposed to address the Breach.

D-6.3 The Processor will cooperate with the Controller in the investigation, mitigation, and any notification to supervisory authorities or data subjects.

### D-7. Audit rights

D-7.1 Once per contract year, on thirty (30) days' written notice, the Controller may audit the Processor's compliance with this Exhibit D during normal business hours and at the Controller's expense.

D-7.2 The Processor will respond to a written security questionnaire from the Controller within fifteen (15) business days.

D-7.3 Audits may not unreasonably interfere with the Processor's operations, and the Processor is not required to disclose Personal Data of other customers or its own confidential commercial information.

### D-8. Return and deletion of Personal Data

D-8.1 On termination or expiry of the Agreement, the Processor will return Personal Data to the Controller in a standard machine-readable format and will delete it within ninety (90) days, unless retention is required by law.

D-8.2 The Processor will certify deletion in writing on the Controller's request.

### D-9. Order of precedence

D-9.1 This Exhibit D prevails over the Agreement to the extent of any conflict relating to the processing of Personal Data.

D-9.2 This Exhibit D does not amend, and may not be relied on to interpret, any commercial term of the Agreement or of any Order Form, including any term relating to fees, performance obligations, or variable consideration.
