## RECITALS

A. Provider develops and operates a cloud-based supply-chain visibility platform marketed under the name Meridian Harbor.
B. Customer wishes to subscribe to the Hosted Services, and Provider is willing to provide them, on the terms and conditions of this Agreement.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Agreement** means this Master Subscription Agreement, together with each Order Form and all exhibits and addenda.
1.2 **Hosted Services** means Provider's hosted software platform described in an Order Form, including Updates, but excluding Implementation Services and Third-Party Services.
1.3 **Order Form** means an ordering document executed by the parties that references this Agreement and identifies the Hosted Services, subscription quantities, fees, and term.
1.4 **Initial Term** has the meaning given in Section 3.1.
1.5 **Renewal Term** has the meaning given in Section 3.2.
1.6 **Subscription Fees** means the fees payable for the Hosted Services as stated in an Order Form.
1.7 **List Price** means Provider's published list price for the applicable Hosted Services in effect at the commencement of the relevant Renewal Term.
1.8 **Contract Year** means each consecutive twelve-month period beginning on the Effective Date or on an anniversary of it.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF THE AGREEMENT

2.1 The parties may execute one or more Order Forms under this Agreement. Each Order Form is governed by this Agreement and is incorporated by reference.
2.2 An executed Order Form becomes effective on the later of the Order Form effective date and the Effective Date of this Agreement.
2.3 A conflict between an Order Form and the body of this Agreement is resolved in the following order of precedence: (a) the Order Form, but only as to fees, quantities, and term; (b) this Agreement; (c) the exhibits.

## 3. TERM

3.1 **Initial Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months (the **Initial Term**), expiring June 30, 2029, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.
3.2 **Renewal Term.** After the Initial Term, this Agreement renews automatically for successive periods of twelve (12) months each (each, a **Renewal Term**), unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the end of the then-current term.
3.3 **Termination for cause.** Either party may terminate this Agreement for the other party's uncured material breach, provided written notice is delivered and the breach remains uncured thirty (30) days after delivery.

<!-- page 3 -->

## 4. FEES AND PAYMENT

4.1 **Subscription Fees.** Customer shall pay the Subscription Fees stated in each Order Form. For Order Form No. 1, the Subscription Fee is $40,000 per month for the Hosted Services.
4.2 **Annual prepayment.** Subscription Fees are invoiced annually in advance. The first invoice of $480,000 is issued on the Effective Date and covers the first Contract Year. Each later annual invoice is issued thirty (30) days before the first day of the applicable Contract Year.
4.3 **Renewal invoices.** Renewal Term Fees are invoiced annually in advance on the same basis as Section 4.2, at the rate determined under Section 5.
4.4 **Late payment.** Undisputed amounts not paid within thirty (30) days of the invoice date accrue interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower.
4.5 **Taxes.** Subscription Fees exclude applicable sales, use, and value-added taxes, which are Customer's responsibility, other than taxes based on Provider's net income.
4.6 **Refunds.** All Subscription Fees are non-refundable except as expressly provided in this Agreement.

## 5. RENEWAL PRICING

5.1 **Automatic renewal.** At the end of the Initial Term and of each Renewal Term, the term renews under Section 3.2.
5.2 **Renewal Fee.** The Subscription Fee for each Renewal Term equals 95% of Provider's then-current List Price for the same configuration of Hosted Services, measured as of the first day of that Renewal Term. The resulting 5% reduction is a renewal discount, not a credit and not a temporary promotion.
5.3 **Application of the renewal discount.** The renewal discount in Section 5.2 applies to every Renewal Term. It does not apply to the Initial Term, and it is not conditioned on any act by Customer other than accepting the automatic renewal.
5.4 **Illustration.** If Provider's List Price on July 1, 2029 is $48,000 per month, the Subscription Fee for the first Renewal Term is $45,600 per month (95% of $48,000), an annual reduction of $28,800 against List Price.
5.5 **Price changes.** Provider may change its List Price at any time, but will notify Customer of any change at least sixty (60) days before the next anniversary of the Effective Date.
5.6 **No stacking.** The renewal discount does not combine with other promotions or discounts unless an Order Form expressly states that it does.

## 6. SUPPORT AND SERVICE LEVELS

6.1 Provider will provide the support tier purchased in an Order Form in accordance with Exhibit A.
6.2 Provider will make the Hosted Services available at least 99.9% of each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
6.3 Any service-level credits are Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for availability failures in a calendar month.

## 7. LIMITATION OF LIABILITY

7.1 Except for gross negligence, wilful misconduct, or breach of confidentiality, each party's aggregate liability under this Agreement is limited to the Subscription Fees paid or payable in the twelve (12) months preceding the claim.
7.2 Neither party is liable for lost profits, loss of data, or indirect, special, or consequential damages.
