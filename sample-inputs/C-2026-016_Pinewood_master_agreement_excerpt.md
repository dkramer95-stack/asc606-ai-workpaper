## RECITALS

A. Provider operates a hosted analytics platform for gaming and sportsbook operators.
B. Customer wishes to subscribe to the platform and to receive a third-party sports and racing data feed that Provider makes available as a reseller of Orbis Data Services, Inc. (**Orbis**).
C. Orbis is an independent third party and is not a party to this Agreement. Orbis supplies the Data Feed directly to Customer through Provider's ordering and invoicing relationship.

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **Platform Subscription** means Provider's hosted analytics platform described in Section 4.
1.2 **Data Feed** means the third-party data feed supplied by Orbis and made available to Customer under this Agreement, including Data Feed Units.
1.3 **Data Feed Units** means the individual data records delivered through the Data Feed.
1.4 **Orbis Rate Card** means the end-customer rate card for the Data Feed published by Orbis from time to time.
1.5 **Orbis Wholesale Charges** means the amounts Provider pays Orbis for the Data Feed.
1.6 **Term** has the meaning given in Section 3.1.

## 2. STRUCTURE OF THE AGREEMENT

2.1 This Agreement covers two offerings: (a) the Platform Subscription, provided by Provider, and (b) the Data Feed, which Provider resells as described in Section 5.
2.2 The Data Feed is not developed, operated, hosted, or controlled by Provider. Provider does not own or license the Data Feed.
2.3 The fees for the two offerings are separately stated in Section 6.

## 3. TERM

3.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for thirty-six (36) months, expiring June 30, 2029, unless terminated earlier under Section 9.
3.2 After the Term, this Agreement renews only by written agreement of the parties.
3.3 Either party may terminate for the other party's uncured material breach on thirty (30) days' written notice.

## 4. PLATFORM SUBSCRIPTION

4.1 Provider grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Platform during the Term for Customer's internal business purposes, including use by Customer's licensed operators.
4.2 Provider will make the Platform available in accordance with the service levels in Exhibit A.
4.3 Provider is solely responsible for the Platform's performance, availability, and support.

## 5. DATA FEED

5.1 **Resale.** Provider makes the Data Feed available to Customer as a reseller. Provider does not supply the Data Feed itself and is not the publisher of the data.
5.2 **Pricing set by Orbis.** The fees payable for the Data Feed are those stated in the Orbis Rate Card, which Orbis publishes and may update from time to time. Provider does not set the end-customer price for the Data Feed and has no discretion to discount it.
5.3 **Orbis terms apply.** The Data Feed is supplied subject to Orbis's standard end-customer terms, which Customer accepts by ordering the Data Feed. Those terms govern the Data Feed and prevail over this Agreement as to the Data Feed.
5.4 **Ordering together.** The Data Feed is ordered together with the Platform Subscription under this Agreement; the two offerings are separately priced and separately described in this Agreement.

## 6. FEES

6.1 The fees payable by Customer over the Term are stated in the fee schedule below and are invoiced monthly in advance at an aggregate of $60,000 per month.

| Component | Fees over the 36-month Term |
|---|---|
| Platform Subscription | $860,000 |
| Data Feed (resold from Orbis) | $1,300,000 |
| **Total fees** | **$2,160,000** |

6.2 Fees exclude applicable taxes, which are Customer's responsibility.
6.3 Fees are non-refundable except as provided in Section 9.

## 7. SERVICE LEVELS AND RESPONSIBILITY

7.1 **Data Feed service levels.** Orbis is solely responsible for the Data Feed's service levels, delivery schedule, and data accuracy. Provider passes through Orbis's service commitments but has no independent obligation to restore, correct, or replace the Data Feed.
7.2 **Data accuracy.** Orbis is solely responsible for the accuracy, completeness, and timeliness of the data in the Data Feed. Provider makes no warranty regarding that data.
7.3 **No control.** Provider does not control the Data Feed, does not take physical possession of it, does not set its specifications, and is not responsible for fulfilment of the Data Feed.
7.4 **Customer remedies.** Customer's remedies for Data Feed failures are against Orbis under Orbis's terms. Provider will use commercially reasonable efforts to facilitate a claim but is not liable for the underlying failure.

## 8. ORBIS WHOLESALE CHARGES

8.1 Provider pays Orbis $980,000 for the Data Feed over the Term.
8.2 **Minimum commitment.** Provider must pay the Orbis Wholesale Charges monthly in arrears regardless of Customer's usage of the Data Feed, including any month in which Customer consumes no Data Feed Units. Provider's obligation to Orbis is not reduced by Customer's non-use or by any Customer dispute.
8.3 **Fixed margin.** Provider's compensation for the Data Feed is fixed at $320,000 over the Term, or $8,888.89 per month. It does not vary with the number of Data Feed Units consumed, with Customer's usage, or with the fees Customer pays for the Platform Subscription.
8.4 **No inventory risk.** Provider bears no inventory risk for the Data Feed and is not entitled to any refund or credit from Orbis based on Customer's use or non-use.

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 On termination for Provider's uncured material breach, Customer is entitled to a pro rata refund of prepaid Platform Subscription fees.
9.2 On any other termination, prepaid fees are non-refundable. Data Feed fees already paid to Orbis are non-refundable.
