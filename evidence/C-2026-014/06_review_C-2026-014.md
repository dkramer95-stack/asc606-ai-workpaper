## Override: material right

The customer receives a 5% discount on every Renewal Term that it would not receive without entering the original contract. That is a material right. Section 5.4 illustrates the discount using a $48,000 list price, which the customer does not control. The option has value even if the customer does not renew, because the right is obtained at contract inception.

Estimate prepared:

- Expected List Price at the first Renewal Term: $48,000 per month (Section 5.4 illustration).
- Annual discount: $48,000 x 12 x 5% = $28,800.
- Expected renewal likelihood: 70%, based on the customer's three-year deployment and the platform's integration into its warehouse operations.
- Standalone selling price of the option: $28,800 x 70% = $20,160.
- Relative SSP allocation: $1,440,000 x $20,160 / ($1,440,000 + $20,160) = $19,882 to the option.
- Subscription allocation: $1,440,000 - $19,882 = $1,420,118.
- Monthly subscription revenue: $1,420,118 / 36 = $39,448.
- FY2026 effect: six months at unrounded monthly revenue of $39,447.72 = $236,686, compared with $240,000 under the model output. Revenue reduction $3,314.

The $19,882 allocated to the material right remains in deferred revenue until the customer exercises or declines the option at the end of the Initial Term (2029-06-30). If the option expires unexercised, the amount is recognized at expiry.
