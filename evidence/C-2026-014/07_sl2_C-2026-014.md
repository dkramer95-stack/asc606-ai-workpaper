## 1. Why this contract was escalated

Two CL-10 triggers applied. Total contract value of $1,440,000 exceeds the $250,000 threshold, and the contract contains an automatic renewal with a price term that differs from list. The model's output also carried the lowest confidence on the contract (0.86) on the material right field, although the confidence score was not the reason for escalation.

## 2. What the first reviewer concluded

The first reviewer confirmed the term, transaction price and single performance obligation, and overrode the model's material right conclusion. The reviewer estimated the option's standalone selling price at $20,160 using a 70% expected renewal likelihood and the $28,800 annual discount implied by the Section 5.4 illustration, then allocated $19,882 of the transaction price to the option. FY2026 revenue was reduced by $3,314.

## 3. What the second reviewer tested

| Step | Performed | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Read the renewal clause in Sections 3.2, 5.2 and 5.3 | Yes | Clause applies a 5% reduction to every Renewal Term |
| Confirmed the customer receives no discount without the original contract | Yes | The discount is conditioned only on automatic renewal |
| Checked the expected-renewal assumption | Yes | 70% is supported by the three-year deployment and warehouse integration described in the customer file; sensitivity at 50% and 90% does not change the conclusion that a material right exists |
| Re-performed the relative SSP allocation | Yes | $19,882 to the option and $1,420,118 to the subscription; agrees |
| Checked the recognition period | Yes | Option consideration is deferred until the option is exercised or expires at 2029-06-30 |
| Checked the journal entry | Yes | Dr Revenue $3,314 / Cr Deferred revenue $3,314; supported |
| Considered whether the renewal discount is a variable consideration issue | Yes | No. The discount is a fixed contractual term, not consideration that varies with uncertain future events |

## 4. Sensitivity

| Renewal likelihood | Option SSP | Allocated to option | FY2026 revenue reduction |
|---|---|---|---|
| 50% | $14,400 | $14,257 | $2,376 |
| 70% (selected) | $20,160 | $19,882 | $3,314 |
| 90% | $25,920 | $25,462 | $4,244 |

The material right conclusion holds across the range. The selected likelihood is the midpoint of the reasonable range and is consistent with the customer's deployment history.

## 5. Conclusion

I concur with the first reviewer. The model's conclusion is rejected and the journal entry is approved. The contract is a single performance obligation with a material right on renewal. The model limitation is logged for the next schema release: require a renewal-price comparison field whenever a contract contains a renewal term at other than contemporaneous list price.
